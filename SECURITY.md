## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a vulnerability in this project, please do the following:

1. **Contact Us**: Send an email to [security@example.com](mailto:security@example.com) with details of the vulnerability.
2. **Response Time**: You can expect an initial response within 48 hours. We will keep you updated on the status of your report as we investigate and resolve the issue.
3. **Resolution**: Once the vulnerability is verified, we will take appropriate steps to mitigate it and release a security update. We will notify you once the issue is resolved.
4. **Disclosure**: We will publicly disclose the details of the vulnerability once it has been resolved, along with crediting the reporter if they wish.
